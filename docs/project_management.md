# Manage a project

Choose **Project → Manage This Project…** or search for it in the command
palette (**Space c c**). The browser opens over the workspace you're inspecting.
At 80 columns, Objects appears above Actions; on wider terminals the lists sit
side by side.

To copy a profile, select it under its chip and flow, then choose **Duplicate
Profile…**. For a chip other than your current launch target, **Browse flows
and profiles…** reads that chip's catalog. Browsing and authoring neither
select the chip for launch nor replace a staged plan.

- Click objects and actions or use **Tab/Shift-Tab** between the lists and
  **↑/↓** or counted **j/k** within a list.
- Click **Filter** or press **/** to filter. The palette commands **Show project
  browser control hints** and **Hide project browser control hints** change a
  persistent preference.
- **Enter** on an object moves to its actions. Completing or cancelling a
  wizard opened here returns to the same object. Dismiss the browser to
  resume your original inspection.

See also [Project workflows](project_workflows.md) and
[Choose the ASIC launch target](launch-target.md).
